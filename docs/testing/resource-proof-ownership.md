## Storage Checkpoint And Accounting Decision

The sync writer now counts through a nonallocating cap-aware sink, reserves once, writes through the existing `CappedWriter`, refuses second-pass growth and shrinkage, and includes its final newline. HTML escaping counts all five substitutions before reserving escaped output. Raw and escaped buffers coexist during transformation. It changes neither successful bytes nor the existing cap. Info/health retain their existing distinct `serialize_json_exact` contract.

Sync final headers are rebuilt only after their current limits pass. Final names/values own bounded bytes, duplicates and sensitivity are retained, and removed fields cannot leave a large final map behind. The old map, new map, response body and cookie can overlap during copying; no claim is made that this also bounds arbitrary caller-owned input maps before the builder is called. `allowed_origin` already copied the matching configured origin and is not replaced with an ingress slice. Provider-added headers remain outside these application limits.

Complete cookie formatting still uses `cookie::Cookie`'s formatter, now writing to a fallibly reserved capped field instead of a growable string. The original preflight, complete 4-KiB field limit, dates, attributes, signed payload and byte-reference tests remain. Its phases differ: date-format scratch overlaps the reserved field during formatting, then dies before conversion; field backing overlaps the copied `HeaderValue` during conversion. Later response decoration can overlap that value with its detached replacement. A physical proof must account for each reachable phase, not invent a simultaneous three-buffer peak or omit the later copy.

Cookie field storage failures have the public `CookieWriteError::Allocation` variant, separate from codec and hard limits. Setuid returns HTTP 500 for that server-side failure and retains HTTP 400 for existing cookie transaction errors, with the same fixed error body and no cookie. The real-App allocator control verifies the status and original receipt lifetime; the exhaustive handler mapping separately pins the unchanged limit responses. This adds a public enum variant; downstream exhaustive matches must handle it. It changes no signed-cookie format, cap or resource charge.

The signed-cookie encoder is a separate input-producing stage, not covered by the formatter's final-field reservation proof. `prepare_cookie_transaction` calls `encode_cookie` on each eviction pass before the 4-KiB field preflight. Its two `BoundedWriter` buffers start empty and grow while retaining raw and HTML-escaped JSON simultaneously (up to the existing decoded-byte limit, 24 KiB by default); base64 encoding and envelope/MAC formatting then construct additional strings. #48 still needs source/allocator/guest bounds for those capacities, copies, overlap and repeated passes. This checkpoint changes neither that encoder nor its limits, and a bounded final cookie is not a proof of bounded physical input construction.

### Terminal Controls

[Ingress controls](../../crates/auctioneer-core/tests/ingress_admission.rs) derive terminal cases from registered auxiliary/probe routes and join route identities back to the SDK router, with explicit framework 404/405 cases. Base responses exercise each SDK completion outcome and abandonment with held auxiliary permits and original work receipts. Separate injected configuration-store failures verify held receipts through error-envelope handoff and drop; they do not repeat the entire outcome cross-product. Pending configuration cancellation proves one bounded read has started before dropping it for every registered auxiliary route, including health. Failed health policy resolution retains its 401 denial and no-store/Bearer headers, while other configuration-dependent routes retain their 503 error contract. Matched non-body collection and both fallback drains exercise body timeout and cancellation before configuration work. Existing [sync route controls](../../crates/auctioneer-core/tests/usersync_routes.rs) also assert held receipts on URL/body/cookie construction refusals before releasing the envelope. Their wire assertions are unchanged.

Additional admitted-App controls reach authorized health JSON and keep its original auxiliary/work receipt through every pinned terminal outcome and abandonment before/after writing. They suspend at a bounded health-token read before cancellation, assert active provider-future destruction and independent probe/static occupancy, then recover. Secret-store failure reaches health's no-store/Bearer denial with the same held receipt. The separate health authorization owners remain required for access-policy semantics; these receipt controls do not certify provider allocation.

The stalled-body control also drives configured cookie-sync, its accepted preflight, getuids and setuid beyond policy resolution into their own body reads. A poll counter proves that the stream actually started before the pending assertions; the body remains held with one auxiliary slot and the full original work receipt. Cancellation destroys the body and releases both counters. An injected body-read timeout returns exactly 408 with `request timeout\n`, retaining both counters until the response is dropped. The matched collector and framework fallback rows still prove they do not read configuration before draining. These controls do not replace sync byte/privacy tests or certify body/provider allocation.

Successful info list, filtered list, base/alias detail and `all` responses now exercise original receipt retention through completion and abandonment with independently varied runtime enable/disable policy. Configured invalid-query and unknown-name errors retain the same original receipt and their exact wire contracts. The metadata-only control discovers its base from the published catalog and uses a synthetic operator alias; no provider protocol or handwritten bidder roster is involved. Sync cancellation controls separately suspend cookie-sync, getuids and setuid at their bounded signing-keyring read, beyond configuration extraction; cancellation, provider failure, and successful recovery retain the disjoint auxiliary, probe and static owners.

The existing [serialization failure owner](../../crates/auctioneer-core/tests/response_serialization.rs) injects a recoverable allocator refusal at each raw JSON, HTML-escaped JSON and complete-cookie reservation. Pure controls independently vary input and reservation sizes, assert the fault actually fired, reject partial output and retries, and retain byte/content positive controls. Its real-App child process reaches all four reservation sites, checks complete handler-specific refusal responses with no cookie, and keeps the original route/work receipts through completion, cancellation and abandonment. The child preserves this target's existing no-envelope controls; no extra integration target or production failure hook is added.

Distinct lifecycle controls abandon the application attempt both before and after writing starts, and assert the observer's corresponding conversion-error or transport-error report. Real SDK fallback selection and preparation retain the original auxiliary permit and work receipt through completion, explicit abort and abandonment before/during writing. The conversion case begins with a genuine framing rejection returning the live attempt; the deadline case selects fallback from a prepared initial attempt, rather than claiming to test clock expiry. Each case asserts exactly one report with the original cause and fallback disposition, plus complete counter recovery.

A fresh-process zero-auxiliary case proves the independent probe-configuration/output and static-refusal owners remain available when auxiliary capacity is zero. Probe output exhaustion/completion controls remain separate; no route acquires a second auxiliary receipt to finish rendering. These are lifetime controls, not certification of the retained containers' physical bytes.

Existing output funding includes two body buffers plus header/cookie storage; the transform and header-rebuild phases differ. That receipt is not a proof of the locked header map's physical layout or of an allocator/host copy multiplier. Native requested-allocation measurements are controls, not guest physical maxima.

**No accounting cutover:** retain `(A + P) * (H * D + T)`, with D the unchanged declared auxiliary work peak, H the existing handoff factor and T transmit state. The candidate `A * (D + (H - 1) * R + T) + P * D` is not implemented. A complete R is unavailable: header-map indices/entries/duplicate storage and allocator slack, cookie scratch/conversion overlap, fallback/info/health/probe shapes, guest layouts and provider handoff/copy contracts are not jointly certified.
